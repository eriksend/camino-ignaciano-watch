84 Ignatian Way practicalities: seasons, costs, water, and accommodation budget
whole · official English Camino Ignaciano — Aspectos prácticos · 2026-06-14 14:39 new

Spring and autumn are recommended seasons; winter brings snow on the Loyola–Logroño section and summer temperatures near Zaragoza can reach 40 °C. Daily costs range from roughly €45–€65: accommodation runs €15–€20 in pilgrim refuges and €30–€50 in hostels/hotels; a tourist dinner menu costs around €15; tap water is described as generally safe. No kitchen facilities should be expected at refuges.
