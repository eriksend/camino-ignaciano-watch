90 Camino Ignaciano practicalities: spring timing, costs, and gear
whole · official English Camino Ignaciano — Aspectos prácticos · 2026-06-11 14:40

Spring and fall are the recommended seasons; summer temperatures in the Zaragoza/Ebro valley reach 40°C, and winter brings snow risk from Loyola to Logroño. Daily costs run €45–€65, with pilgrim refuges at €15–€20 and hotels at €30–€50; the Los Monegros stretch between Zaragoza and Fraga has limited accommodation and should be planned carefully. Gear recommendations include broken-in shoes, rain poncho, sun protection, and a pack no heavier than 10% of body weight.
